# Research Lessons

- Searching for common names ("Daniel Cerezo") heavily surfaces homonyms and requires careful disambiguation using specific project names ("Creer Hacer", "TEDxRiodelaPlata").
- Finding explicitly open-licensed portrait photography for living individuals is difficult without direct contact or dedicated press kits.
- Canonical sources (major newspapers, TED) explicitly prohibit or do not grant commercial/redistribution rights for their imagery.
- Improved prompt clause: "When researching individuals without official press kits, prioritize explicit open licenses (e.g., Wikimedia Commons with verified subjects) and clearly document homonym disambiguation."
