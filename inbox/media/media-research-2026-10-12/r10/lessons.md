# Lessons

- Always verify image rights explicitly. Simply because an image is hosted on an official website does not grant a promotional reuse license. When ambiguous, use `reference_only` and do not download.
- Improved Prompt Clause: "When discovering visual assets on an official organization website, inspect the site's footer and terms for explicit open licenses (e.g. Creative Commons) or explicit press kit terms. Without these, treat all assets as `reference_only` and do not download them locally."
